CodeCollab

A social platform for developers to share code snippets and collaborate

The lack of a dedicated platform for developers to share and collaborate on code snippets

  • 75% of developers spend an average of 2 hours per day searching for code snippets and debugging existing code

Over 24 million developers worldwide, with a potential user base of 10 million developers (40% of the total developer population)

Revenue potential of $100 million per year

CodeCollab provides a seamless experience for developers to connect, share knowledge, and work together on coding projects
